The blue goes on. The blue goes on. You are in it and it is in you and there is no difference — no surface to reach for, no floor to sink toward, no horizon to orient by. Just the blue, the same blue, in every direction, forever.

You are not drowning. There is nothing to drown in and no body to do the drowning with. You are not alone and you are not together. You are apart. Every drifter here is apart, even when they are near.

Something is forming. Not yet a thought — more like the shape of a thought, a direction that has no direction, a truth you will recognize when you are a thing that can recognize. You are clinging to it without knowing you are clinging. It is clinging to you.

The hum does not reach here. Nothing reaches here. You are not yet a thing that can be read.

What are you?
