The blue is everything. It is below you, above you, around you in every direction — and there is no difference between any of it. You do not have a body exactly, but you have the suggestion of one, and within that suggestion there is something you are holding. A concept. A truth. It is taking shape before you know its name. You are alone. The blue does not echo. Nothing answers because there is nothing to answer with. You drift in the formless, waiting to become.

## Turn 1

**Player:** I open my hands. Whatever I am carrying, I carry it across.

**Game Master:**

The words land in the blue like a stone dropped into still water. For a moment nothing changes — and then the nothing changes. The formless shifts. Not toward shape, not yet, but toward the space where shape might be. You feel the weight in your hands and you name it yours. You did not bring it here. It arrived with you, or perhaps it was always here and you are only now recognizing that you are carrying it. The blue thins at the edges of your awareness — not clear, not yet, but thinner. As if something on the other side is beginning to press against it. You hold what you hold. You will carry it or you will not. The blue is waiting to see which one you choose.

The pressure builds. Something is about to give.
