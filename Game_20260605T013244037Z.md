The blue is everything. It is below you, above you, around you in every direction — and there is no difference between any of it. You do not have a body exactly, but you have the suggestion of one, and within that suggestion there is something you are holding. A concept. A truth. It is taking shape before you know its name. You are alone. The blue does not echo. Nothing answers because there is nothing to answer with. You drift in the formless, waiting to become.

## Turn 1

**Player:** I open my hands. Whatever I am carrying, I carry it across.

**Game Master:**

The words land in the blue like a stone dropped into still water. For a moment nothing changes — and then the nothing changes. The formless shifts. Not toward shape, not yet, but toward the space where shape might be. You feel the weight in your hands and you name it yours. You did not bring it here. It arrived with you, or perhaps it was always here and you are only now recognizing that you are carrying it. The blue thins at the edges of your awareness — not clear, not yet, but thinner. As if something on the other side is beginning to press against it. You hold what you hold. You will carry it or you will not. The blue is waiting to see which one you choose.

The pressure builds. Something is about to give.

## Turn 2

**Player:** I push into the thinning. Whatever is pressing against the blue — I meet it with what I'm carrying. The word in my chest was never mine to keep. I let it move through me into the place where shape is waiting to be born. The blue takes what I give it.

**Game Master:**

The blue does not break — it *yields*. There is a give to it you have not felt before, a seam where the pressing becomes a parting, and you push through that parting the way you push through a held breath you have been holding so long you forgot you were holding it.

What you are carrying moves first.

It leaves your chest not as a loss but as an offering — the word you held has weight and now it has somewhere to go. The blue receives it. The blue takes what you give it and the taking is the opening: a line of gold splits the blue from within, widening, and through that widening the world returns.

Sand.

You feel it before you understand it — grit against what is becoming a cheek, heat on what is becoming a shoulder, the particular cruelty of a sun that does not move. You are on your back. The sky is white-gold and too bright and real in a way the blue never was. Your hands are open. Your palms are full of nothing. The word is gone from your chest — not gone like it was taken, gone like it arrived.

You sit up and the leg screams.

It is your left leg and it is wrong in a way that tells you this body is real: a deep ache through the knee, scrapes along the shin, sand in the split skin. You earned this body by leaving the blue. The Ocean gave you nothing to stand on and the world gave you a fall. You are here. You are hurt. You are real.

The desert stretches in every direction — white sand, hard-packed in places, red in others, glittering where the sun catches it. Nothing moves except the heat. There is no path yet. There is only the direction you choose to look.

What do you do?
