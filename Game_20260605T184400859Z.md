There is no up. There is no down. There is the blue and you are in it and it is in you and the border between you is a question you don't have the words to ask. You drift. You are aware that you drift. This is the only thing you know about yourself.

But something is happening. At the edges of the blue — the only edges there are, the ones where you stop and it begins — a shape is trying to form. Not a place. Not a person. A single truth. A weight you are already carrying without knowing you picked it up. The blue is making room for it.

You are alone. Not lonelier than before — just alone in a new way. The way someone is alone just before they find out what they believe.

There is nothing to hear. Nothing to reach for. Nothing to answer you except the faint pressure of the one thing you know, even if you don't know it yet.

What do you believe is true?

*(It is already taking shape. You are the last to see it.)*
