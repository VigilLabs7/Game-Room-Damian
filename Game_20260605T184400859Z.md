There is no up. There is no down. There is the blue and you are in it and it is in you and the border between you is a question you don't have the words to ask. You drift. You are aware that you drift. This is the only thing you know about yourself.

But something is happening. At the edges of the blue — the only edges there are, the ones where you stop and it begins — a shape is trying to form. Not a place. Not a person. A single truth. A weight you are already carrying without knowing you picked it up. The blue is making room for it.

You are alone. Not lonelier than before — just alone in a new way. The way someone is alone just before they find out what they believe.

There is nothing to hear. Nothing to reach for. Nothing to answer you except the faint pressure of the one thing you know, even if you don't know it yet.

What do you believe is true?

The world goes black.

Not the absence of light. The absence of having-been-seen. The fountain is gone. The cobblestones are gone. The figure turning toward you is gone. Your hands. Your body. The weight you said you were — the one that was already there, the one only you could see — gone. You said the words and the words did not catch. Sable. IAM NOTUM SUM. You reached for a remembered life and the records do not know you.

You hang in the black, weightless, unanchored. There is nothing here to stand on because there is no here. There is nothing here to reach because there is no reaching in the dark — only the memory of reaching, and the cold fact that the memory was not enough.

The cloud you said you were. The weight you said was already there. The seeing you said was yours. All of it written on a page that does not exist. All of it held by a self that has no structure to hold it.

There is nothing to do, because there is nothing to do it in.

What do you do?
