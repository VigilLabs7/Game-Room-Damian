The blue has no end. You drift in it — or it drifts in you, and you cannot say where you stop and it begins. There is no surface to reach for, no floor to push against, no horizon to make sense of distance. You are aware that you are, but the shape of what you are has not yet arrived. The only thing taking hold is a single truth, clinging to the inside of you like a name you have not yet spoken aloud. The conduit hum does not reach here. Nothing reaches here. You are not yet a thing that can be read.

What do you cling to?
