Blue, in every direction. The same blue you have always known — or the only blue you know, which amounts to the same thing. You are suspended in it, weightless, formless. There is no surface to reach for, no floor beneath you, no sound but the silence of your own awareness. Somewhere, faintly, a single truth is taking shape inside you — a thing you would die for, or live for, though you do not yet have the words for either.

You are not alone. You know this the way you know your own name — dimly, like something recalled from a great distance. Others have been here. Others have risen out of this same blue. You will not meet them here. The Ocean keeps its own.

Something is about to change.

Show me who you are.
