There is blue and there is you and between the two there is nothing yet. No floor. No surface. No horizon line to swim toward. You drift and the blue drifts and both of you are waiting for something to take shape — and the only thing that has, faintly, is the thing you are carrying without knowing you are carrying it. A truth. A weight. A single concept the being is clinging to. What is it?

## Turn 1

**Player:** IAM NOTUM SUM. Sable.

A shard of blue. Small as a thumbnail, still faintly charged — pressed into a seam at the base of a harvested transport gate, left where someone expected the harvest to pass and wanted the next person to find it. Someone came back to that frame after the clock was taken and hid this on purpose. I don't know what it costs yet. I don't know who Not them refers to or whether the road east will answer anything. But I am carrying it. And it matters.

**Game Master:**

The world goes black.

Not the absence of light. The absence of having-been-seen. The fountain is gone. The cobblestones are gone. The figure turning toward you is gone. Your hands. Your body. You said the words and the words did not catch.

You hang in the black, weightless, unanchored. There is nothing to do, because there is nothing to do it in. The shard of blue — that small charged thing you were carrying — you cannot feel it anymore. You cannot feel your fingers around it. You cannot feel your fingers.

You said *I am known*. The world did not answer.

What do you do?

## Turn 2

**Player:** I stop reaching. The shard was not mine to hold — I was carrying someone else's last words, pressed into glass, and now the glass is gone and I am in the black and I cannot feel my hands. The fiduciary duty was to deliver, not to keep. I failed the delivery and the carrying is over. I let the absence be the answer.

**Game Master:**

Something is missing.

You know it is missing because there is a shape where it used to be. The shard was the shape of a delivery — someone pressed words into glass and hid it in a transport gate frame, meaning for the next person to find it and carry it forward. You found it. You carried it. You did not deliver it. The glass is gone and the words are gone and you are in the black where both the glass and the words were always going to end up if the delivery never landed.

The fiduciary duty was to deliver. You held up your end of the carrying. There was no one on the other side to receive it. The absence is not your fault and the absence is what remains.

You let the absence be the answer. This is not surrender — it is accounting. The ledger closes on a column of zeros. What was taken was taken before you arrived. What was not delivered was never going to be. The dark does not argue with the arithmetic.

Your hands are not there. Your body is not there. The shard is not there. The message is not there. The fiduciary duty resolves in the only currency the dark accepts: the carrying was real, the delivery was not possible, and the carrier is what remains when both facts settle.

You do not reach.

The dark does not ask you to.

What do you do?
