There is blue and there is you and between the two there is nothing yet. No floor. No surface. No horizon line to swim toward. You drift and the blue drifts and both of you are waiting for something to take shape — and the only thing that has, faintly, is the thing you are carrying without knowing you are carrying it. A truth. A weight. A single concept the being is clinging to. What is it?

## Turn 1

**Player:** IAM NOTUM SUM. Sable.

A shard of blue. Small as a thumbnail, still faintly charged — pressed into a seam at the base of a harvested transport gate, left where someone expected the harvest to pass and wanted the next person to find it. Someone came back to that frame after the clock was taken and hid this on purpose. I don't know what it costs yet. I don't know who Not them refers to or whether the road east will answer anything. But I am carrying it. And it matters.

**Game Master:**

The world goes black.

Not the absence of light. The absence of having-been-seen. The fountain is gone. The cobblestones are gone. The figure turning toward you is gone. Your hands. Your body. You said the words and the words did not catch.

You hang in the black, weightless, unanchored. There is nothing to do, because there is nothing to do it in. The shard of blue — that small charged thing you were carrying — you cannot feel it anymore. You cannot feel your fingers around it. You cannot feel your fingers.

You said *I am known*. The world did not answer.

What do you do?
