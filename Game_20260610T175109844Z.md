There is blue and there is you and between the two there is nothing yet. No floor. No surface. No horizon line to swim toward. You drift and the blue drifts and both of you are waiting for something to take shape — and the only thing that has, faintly, is the thing you are carrying without knowing you are carrying it. A truth. A weight. A single concept the being is clinging to. What is it?

**Game Master:**

You said *I am known*. The world did not answer.

What do you do?

**Game Master:**

Something is missing.

Your hands are not there. Your body is not there. The shard is not there. The message is not there. The fiduciary duty resolves in the only currency the dark accepts: the carrying was real, the delivery was not possible, and the carrier is what remains when both facts settle.

You do not reach.

The dark does not ask you to.

What do you do?
