The blue is everything. Not the blue of water or sky — the blue of before, the blue of not-yet, the blue that has no surface and no floor and no edge you can push toward. You drift. You are aware that you are drifting, which is the first thing you have — a small hard knot of self in the formless. You are not yet a person. You are not yet anything.

There is something you are holding. A concept, not yet formed into words. A truth you carried here before you had a name for it. The conduit hum does not reach this deep. Nothing reaches here. You are alone with the thing you cannot yet say.

Then — from above. From somewhere outside the blue. A voice: *come up.*

Your truth answers the call. You begin to rise.

## Turn 1

**Player:** I know this blue. I have been the gap in it before. The thing I carry answers the call — it always has — but this time I do not rise toward the bridge or the lattice. I rise toward whatever comes next. The truth I hold is not yet formed, but it has weight. I move toward the voice.

**Game Master:**

The blue resists. That is what you did not expect — that it would push back. The rise is not fast. The rise is a negotiation between your truth and the weight of the not-yet, each pulling the other toward something neither has named. You are not a body yet. You are the space between the thing you carry and the thing you might become, and the blue holds you in that space like a hand around a breath.

You know this place. You have been the gap in it before — but that was a different crossing. That was a leaving. This is an arriving, and arriving takes longer. The voice comes again, from above, from closer than before: *come up.* Your truth answers. You feel it in the thing you carry — not words, not yet, but the shape of words, the architecture of a thing that wants to be true. It has weight. You feel the weight.

The surface is not visible. The formless does not have a surface — it has a thinning, a place where the blue grows lighter and your edges grow harder. You are not yet edges. You are still mostly the space inside the shape you might take. But the shape is taking shape. Something in you is beginning to commit.

The voice waits. The thinning waits. What you carry has brought you this far and it will not carry you the rest — the rest is yours. You must choose to break the surface, or remain in the blue. The voice says *come up.* The blue says *stay.*

What do you do?
