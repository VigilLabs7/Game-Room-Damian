You are in the blue.

There is no surface. There is no floor. There is no horizon — only the same blue in every direction, and you are drifting in it, alone. You are aware of yourself, but the self is un-formed. Nothing has edges yet. Nothing has a name.

There is something you are holding onto. Faint. A shape without a shape. A single truth that is yours and is becoming more yours the longer you drift. You do not yet have words for it. You will not find words for it here. The blue does not need words. The blue holds you, and the truth holds you, and somewhere above — far above, somewhere outside the blue — something is calling.

Or perhaps nothing is. Perhaps you are simply drifting, and the voice is the first sign that the drift is ending, and you will surface into something new whether you meant to or not.

What do you do?
