The blue has no bottom. You know this the way you know your own name — not because anyone told you, but because the knowing was always already there, the way water is always already around you. You drift in it. There is no current, no warmth, no voice. Only the blue, and the faintest sense that something is taking shape inside you — a single truth you are gripping without knowing you are gripping anything yet. You are not yet a person. You are not yet a self. You are the moment before all of that.

Something shifts. A pressure from above — not a sound, not a light, but a *reaching*. Someone is looking for you. Someone is calling something true into the blue and the truth is catching on the thing inside you that is almost a self.

You.

What do you reach for?
