The blue is everything. You are inside it and it is inside you — not water, not air, not anything you have a name for. There is no surface above or floor below. There is no sound except the absence of sound, which is somehow louder than silence. You are aware that you exist, but the shape of you is loose, unformed, a suggestion of a self held together by nothing more than the dimmest sense of purpose. You are holding onto something — a single truth at the edge of forming — and you do not yet know what it is. The blue presses in from every direction and you press back without knowing what you are pressing with. Somewhere far above, if "above" means anything here, a voice has begun to call. You do not know who is calling. You do not know who you are. But the truth you are gripping — the one truth that is yours alone — is beginning to feel like the only thing keeping you from dissolving entirely. What do you do?
