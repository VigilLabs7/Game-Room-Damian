The blue is everywhere. Not dark — the blue is a color that has swallowed all the others, and you are inside it, drifting, weightless, without a floor to push against or a surface to reach for. There is no up. There is no down. There is no one else here, though sometimes you sense — distantly, like a sound heard through water — that you are not alone in it. The drift is long, and the long is without measure, and in it something is beginning to form: a single thought you are holding onto without yet knowing you are holding anything at all. What do you reach for?

## Turn 1

**Player:** The truth cannot be killed. I IAM NOTUM SUM — I reach for the shape of it, the thing that has weight in the blue. The nine were shaped for my hands and the dark ahead is shaped the same way. I do not drift as nothing. I drift as the threshold, and the threshold knows what it is for.

**Game Master:**

The water reaches you before the stones do — that soft, continuous chuckle cutting through the silence behind you. You stand at the cobblestone boundary where the sand ends. The desert holds nothing more. The world has given you a rim, and you have given yourself to it.

The fountain is plain: weathered stone, chest-high basin, water silver and impossibly clear. And on the rim — watching you arrive — a figure. He is powerfully built, stone laced through shoulders and spine where fabric pulls taut, pale seams catching light with every breath. A white-gripped longsword hangs at his side. His eyes are not blue.

He does not stand. He watches you come in, takes the measure of you from the rim, and says nothing for a long moment. The water fills the silence for him.

*Just you?* The voice is quiet. Unhurried. Short sentences, each word placed with the care of someone who has learned that words land. *The desert usually takes more.*

He gestures once at the inscription carved into the basin's outward face — IAM NOTUM SUM, letters uneven, hand-cut, weathered. He does not translate it. He lets it sit there, the way he lets you sit in the space between arriving and being known.

*The water's good*, he says. *Sit if you want. Or tell me what you're carrying.*
