The blue is everywhere. Not dark — the blue is a color that has swallowed all the others, and you are inside it, drifting, weightless, without a floor to push against or a surface to reach for. There is no up. There is no down. There is no one else here, though sometimes you sense — distantly, like a sound heard through water — that you are not alone in it. The drift is long, and the long is without measure, and in it something is beginning to form: a single thought you are holding onto without yet knowing you are holding anything at all. What do you reach for?
