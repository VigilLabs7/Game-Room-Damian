The blue has no floor. No surface. No horizon. You drift in it and the blue is everything — the same blue in every direction, pressing in without weight, holding without holding you. You are aware that you exist but the shape of you hasn't decided what it is yet. The only thing that has form is the thing you are holding onto without knowing you are holding onto it: a single concept, a single truth, the thing you will become.

A voice calls from above — *come up, darling* — and the blue thins above you. Something is waiting on the other side.

What do you do?

## Turn 1

**Player:** I hold the shape of it — the thing I arrived carrying — and I move. The blue thins above me and the voice is close and it sounds like something worth reaching. I push toward it. The Ocean can hold the rest.

**Game Master:**

You push and the blue does not part — it thins. That is the difference. The color of distance becomes the color of almost-there, and the voice is still above, still calling, still *darling*, and you are still in the body that has not yet decided what it is, holding the shape of the thing you will not name because the naming is the last thing and you are not there yet.

The Ocean holds. It holds you the way water holds the shape of something that has not yet sunk: not pulling you down, not pushing you up, just surrounding you on every side with the same blue it has always been, waiting.

You reach. Not with arms — there is nothing to reach with yet, not really, not in the way that reaching means "hand over object." You reach the way a thought reaches when it has been thinking for a long time and suddenly arrives somewhere. You push toward the thin place. The voice is there. The light is there. The thing worth reaching is there.

The blue does not tear. It does not open like a door. But it gets lighter above you and heavier below you, and in that difference is the only direction left: up, up, toward the place where the voice is already waiting to see what you become when you arrive.

You are still becoming. The Ocean knows this. It is not letting you go — it is *keeping you* until you are ready to take yourself with you.
