The blue has no end. No floor, no surface, no horizon — only the same in every direction, and you in it, or the idea of you, drifting. You are not cold. You are not warm. You are not anything yet, except the thing you are holding: a single truth you cannot name because naming would mean knowing what it is, and you do not yet. You only know it is yours.

The blue thins somewhere above you. Not a direction — the blue itself becomes less itself, and through the thinning comes something that might be a voice, might be a silence shaped like a voice, calling upward. Your truth catches on it. A log in water. Something growing branches.

What do you reach for?
