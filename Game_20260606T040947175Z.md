The blue is everything. Before you, nothing. Behind you, nothing. There is no surface to reach for, no floor to push against — only the same blue in every direction, and the slow awareness that you are here, that you exist, that something inside you is holding on even though you cannot yet name what it is.

You drift. You do not know for how long. There is no time here that works the way time works in the world above — only the sense of being, of persisting, of not yet being gone. And then, faintly, something begins to take shape. A concept. A principle. A single truth that is becoming more real the longer you hold it — not yet a word, not yet a name, but the shape of what you will carry when the blue lets go.

A voice finds you. Not from below. From somewhere above the blue entirely — warm, deliberate, intimate. *Come up, darling.* Your ideatum catches the call like a hand catching a rope. You feel yourself beginning to rise. The blue thins. The color bleeds toward white, toward gold, toward heat.

You break the surface into something new. Sand presses against skin you did not know you had. A sun the size of a judgment burns straight down from a sky with no memory of clouds. The world is here. It has been waiting.

You are what you carried up with you. What is that?

**What do you do?**

**Game Master:**

The world goes black.

The sun at the apex. You said the words and the words did not catch. Sable — you reached for that name like a rope and the dark had no hand to offer.

You hang in the black, weightless, unanchored. There is nothing to do, because there is nothing to do it in. No Lagota on the horizon. No stones in your coat. No H. Voss tag to read. The question you carry — *Not them* — has no air to travel in. There is no air.

The reading stopped.

You are not sure how you know.

But you are still here. Still holding. Still asking the dark to explain what it swallowed.
