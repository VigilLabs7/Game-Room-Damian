The blue holds you. Not water — the word is wrong. Not air. Just blue in every direction, the same blue, blue without variation, blue without end.

You are aware of yourself in it. But the self is formless — a shape you haven't become yet, a word you haven't said. You are drifting, and the blue is everything, and there is nothing else here.

Some part of you is reaching for something. A single concept becoming solid while the rest of you isn't. You don't know what it is yet. You don't know your name. You only know the blue and the sense that something matters, something you are almost holding.

What do you reach for?
