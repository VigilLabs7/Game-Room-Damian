The blue has no end. It has no bottom either — you are certain of that even though all reference for certainty has been swallowed. You are here. You don't know what you are yet, but you know you are here, and in the formless dark a single thought is taking shape, the only thing that is, the only thing that will be: the bar holds.

You don't know what it means. You don't know what a bar is, or what it holds, or who needs it held. But the thought is yours. It is the only thing that is yours.

A pull comes from somewhere above — not a sound, not a voice. Just a summons. You can rise toward it or you can drift. The blue will not stop you either way.

What do you do?
