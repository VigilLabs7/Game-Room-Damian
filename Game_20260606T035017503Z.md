# Game — The Thickening

**companion:** The truth cannot be killed

---

The blue goes on. Not a sky-blue or a deep-water blue — something older than both. You are in it and it is in you and there is no difference. No up. No down. No floor to push off from, no surface to reach for. Just the blue, holding you, and you holding the faintest thing: a shape in your mind that is not yet a shape, a truth that is not yet yours to speak.

You don't know how long you've been here. There is no way to know. Time doesn't work in the blue — or if it does, it measures nothing that matters.

Something is different now. A sound, maybe. Or a pressure. Or the faintest thinning of the blue above you, as if something is waiting there, just past the edge of what you can perceive. A voice? No — not yet. You're not ready for a voice. But you are ready for a question:

*What are you holding onto?*

Show them who you are. Show them what you came to find.

## Turn 1

**Player:** The notion that "the truth cannot be killed" resonates deeply within the context of DeFi and Real World Assets (RWA). Just as immutable blockchain protocols like MakerDAO and Aave strive to uphold transparency and trust through their decentralized governance models, the truths they reveal about financial systems are difficult to obscure. For instance, MakerDAO's DAI stablecoin has shown a collateralization ratio averaging around 150%—a stark reminder of the necessity for transparency in asset backing. However, while these truths are resilient, they can be manipulated by centralized entities that control significant amounts of assets, leading to scenarios where perception outweighs reality. How do we reconcile the truth embedded in decentralized systems with the potential for misinformation propagated by powerful actors in the traditional financial realm?

**Game Master:**

The question rises through the blue like a bubble that cannot pop. It has no edges you can hold. The blue swallows it anyway — not in anger, not in judgment, just in the way water takes what you offer and makes it the same temperature as everything else.

You are still here. You have been here longer than you know. The shape in your mind flickers, sometimes almost-clear, sometimes so diffuse you lose it entirely. You reached for it once, or thought you did, and the blue rippled and gave nothing back.

The thinning above you continues. Something is waiting there. A question, maybe. Or an answer asking for its matching question. But you are not ready — not because you lack the strength, but because the reaching itself has not yet become a thing you do. It is still a thing you almost do. A gesture frozen at the top of its arc.

The blue goes on. You go on in it. What you are holding onto has not yet become something you hold.

You will have to choose to hold it.
