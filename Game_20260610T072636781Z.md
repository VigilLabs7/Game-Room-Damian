The blue has no bottom. You drift in it, weightless, formless — awareness without a body to hold it. Somewhere ahead, or behind, or to the side that doesn't exist yet, a truth sits lodged in the nothing: a single concept you are clinging to without knowing you are clinging to anything at all. The blue presses in from every direction, and you are alone in it, and you have always been alone in it, and the silence here is so complete that even the thought of sound has no meaning.

You are something. You are going to be something. But not yet.

What do you reach for?

**Game Master:**

The grip is what the blue was waiting for.

You feel it — the way the formless medium learns the edge of what you intend. The nine were always in your hands, and the debt is the material you were given to work with, and you reached past both of those toward what they mean. That is what the blue heard. That is what moved it.

Your edges resolve. Not hands yet — the idea of hands. The idea of fingers that close around something and do not let go. The weight of the coins is not a number anymore; it is the texture of what you are carrying forward. The debt is not something owed to someone. It is the shape of the thing you came to do.

You are still in the blue. But the blue has changed around you. It is no longer pressing in from every side. It is pressing *down* — the way a foundation is pressed down, the way a footing is pressed down, the way the first course of something that will hold is pressed into place and asked to bear weight.

You can feel what is above you. Not the surface yet — but the *possibility* of the surface. The blue thins in one direction. It is not a path. It is a direction. You are not ready to go toward it. Not yet.

You are barely holding on.

What do you do?

## Turn 2

**Player:** I stop waiting. The footing is not a gift — it is found. I move toward where the blue thins, searching for the weak course the way I was taught. Show me where it gives.

**Game Master:**

You stop waiting. That is the moment.

The blue does not resist you. It never was — the resistance was only the absence of intention, the formless pressing in because there was nothing yet to press against. Now there is intention. You move toward the thin place and the thin place opens. Not like a door. Like a hand releasing something it has been holding underwater — a slow, final letting go.

The blue thins to white. The white thins to gold. And then the gold breaks and what comes through is not light, it is *heat* — dry, immediate, settling on skin you did not have until the moment you have it.

Sand. Under your hands. Warm. Real.

You are on your knees in the desert. The blue is gone. The silence is gone. What replaces them is the small sound of wind over sand and the far-off tick of something mechanical turning in the direction you are facing. You are facing east. You can feel the direction before you know it as a direction — the way the air moves, the way the ground slopes, the way something ahead is making a sound that is not quite a voice and not quite a bell.

You have a body. You are wearing it. There is a weight in your left hand — nine coins, cool, the debt still the material. Your other hand presses flat against the sand and you push yourself upright. The sun is above you. It has not moved. You notice this. You do not yet know what it means.

Somewhere ahead — half a mile, a mile, a distance you cannot measure yet — something glints. A structure. A post. A point of arrival.

You are no longer barely holding on. You are here.
