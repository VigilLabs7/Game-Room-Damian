Somewhere ahead, or behind, or to the side that doesn't exist yet, a truth sits lodged in the nothing: a single concept you are clinging to without knowing you are clinging to anything at all. The blue presses in from every direction, and you are alone in it, and you have always been alone in it, and the silence here is so complete that even the thought of sound has no meaning.

You are something. You are going to be something. But not yet.

What do you reach for?

You are no longer barely holding on. You are here.
