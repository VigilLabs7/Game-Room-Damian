The blue is without edges. Not empty — the blue itself is the substance, and it fills everything. You are in it without being a body in it. There is no surface to reach for. No floor to push off from. No air anywhere. Just the blue, and the faint knowledge that you are, which is the most you have right now.

Something is taking shape at the center of you. A single concept, still undefined — the shape of what you will be when the blue decides to let you go. You can feel it solidifying, log-like, structural. This is the thing you will carry into whatever comes next.

A voice reaches down from somewhere outside the blue. Not directions. Not instructions. A presence that sees you and calls upward. *Come up.* The ideatum answers being seen. The log grows branches. You begin to rise.

The blue thins. Something separates — light from dark, warmth from cold, the faint hum of stone from the silence of nowhere. You are becoming a thing with edges.

What are you reaching for?
