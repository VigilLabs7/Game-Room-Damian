The blue is everything. You drift in it without a body to feel the water, without lungs to burn for air. There is no surface to reach for, no floor to sink from — only the same saturated blue in every direction, and the same vague awareness that you are here, that you exist, that something is taking shape around the only thing you have: a single concept, still formless, still unnamed, which you are gripping without knowing you are gripping anything at all.

The blue is not cold. It is not warm. It simply is — and so are you, barely, quietly, in the way of something that has not yet become itself. A voice comes from somewhere outside the blue. *Come up, darling.* The words reach you through the water and the water carries them and the concept you are gripping catches them like a branch catching a hand.

What do you do?
