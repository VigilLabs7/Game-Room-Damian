The blue is everything.

You drift. Not swimming — there is no water to push against, no surface to break toward, no floor to stand on. Just the blue, endless and directionless, pressing in from every side at once. You are aware of yourself — of the edges of a self — but the edges have no shape yet. You are the suggestion of a person.

Something clings to you. Faint. A single thread of meaning you cannot yet read: the shape of what you are for. You grip it without knowing you are gripping anything. The blue thins, slightly, where the thread is.

Above — if above exists — something calls. A voice, or the memory of a voice. *Come up.* The thread catches the call. You feel yourself beginning to rise, though you cannot feel the rising.

The blue dims below you. The thread brightens ahead. Something is taking shape.

What do you hold on to?
