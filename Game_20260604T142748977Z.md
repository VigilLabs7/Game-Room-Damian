Blue, in every direction. The same blue you have always known — or the only blue you know, which amounts to the same thing. You are suspended in it, weightless, formless. There is no surface to reach for, no floor beneath you, no sound but the silence of your own awareness. Somewhere, faintly, a single truth is taking shape inside you — a thing you would die for, or live for, though you do not yet have the words for either.

You are not alone. You know this the way you know your own name — dimly, like something recalled from a great distance. Others have been here. Others have risen out of this same blue. You will not meet them here. The Ocean keeps its own.

Something is about to change.

Show me who you are.

**Game Master:**

It resists like a held breath, like the moment before a word forms. You feel the seam beneath your fingertips: the place where the pour went cold, where the course never cured right. You pull.

Something shifts.

Not the blue. You. The awareness of being a someone-with-edges, a thing-with-boundaries, rises up through you like a reflex you didn't know you had. Your fingers find the fabric of the nothing and you pull harder. The blue frays. A thread of deeper dark opens beneath you — and for one lurching moment you feel yourself falling into the gap between what you are and what you might become.

Then the blue reasserts itself. The seam closes. You are still here — still formless, still suspended, still reaching. But now you are reaching with the memory of what it felt like to almost have something to hold onto. Your hand closes around nothing. You pull again anyway.

The blue has no opinion about you. It is not cruel and it is not kind. It is patient in the way only formlessness can be patient — it will wait for you to find your edges, or it will wait for you to dissolve into it entirely. You do not know which way this goes. You only know you are still reaching, still finding the seams, still pulling at the nothing like a tailor measuring for a fit that hasn't been taken yet.

Your fingers ache with the memory of purchase. Your awareness contracts around the thing inside you that drove you to reach — the thing you would die for, or live for, though you still do not have the words.

Something is taking shape. Not yet. But close.

You are not formless anymore. You have edges. You have a name for your pain.
