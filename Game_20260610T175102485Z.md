The blue goes on. Not a color — a condition. You are in it and it is in you and neither of you has edges yet. Somewhere far above there might be air but you cannot reach it and you are not sure you want to. The only thing that has form is the thing you are holding without knowing you are holding it. A single truth. A single weight. What are you?

## Turn 1

**Player:** The weight. Not the blue — what I'm carrying in it. A shard small enough to hide in a fist, charged with someone's last message. A question that won't come back. The mask keeps what it hides. IAM NOTUM SUM — Sable. I reach for the keeper's truth.

**Game Master:**

The world goes black.

Not the absence of light. The absence of having-been-seen.

The fountain is gone. The cobblestones are gone. The figure turning toward you is gone. Your hands. Your body. You said the words and the words did not catch.

You hang in the black, weightless, unanchored. There is nothing to do, because there is nothing to do it in.

**What do you do?**

## Turn 2

**Player:** I do not wait for the dark to explain itself. I press my hand forward into the nothing — feeling for the edge of it, for the weight that brought me here, for the shard that hasn't left me. If the words didn't catch, something else will.

**Game Master:**

Something is missing.

You know it is missing because there is a shape where it used to be. Your fingers close on nothing and you understand that nothing is not the same as absence — nothing is the place where something was taken back. The shard. You were carrying a shard. It held a message, a question, someone's last word pressed into glass. You remember the weight of it. You cannot remember the words.

You reach again. Deeper. The dark does not push back. It simply continues.

There is no edge to find. No keeper. No truth waiting to be held. The IAM NOTUM SUM was supposed to catch — you felt it rise in your throat and leave your mouth and the world was supposed to answer. The world did not answer. You are still here, still reaching, still holding the shape of what was taken.

**What do you do?**
