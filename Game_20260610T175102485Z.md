The blue goes on. Not a color — a condition. You are in it and it is in you and neither of you has edges yet. Somewhere far above there might be air but you cannot reach it and you are not sure you want to. The only thing that has form is the thing you are holding without knowing you are holding it. A single truth. A single weight. What are you?

## Turn 1

**Player:** The weight. Not the blue — what I'm carrying in it. A shard small enough to hide in a fist, charged with someone's last message. A question that won't come back. The mask keeps what it hides. IAM NOTUM SUM — Sable. I reach for the keeper's truth.

**Game Master:**

The world goes black.

Not the absence of light. The absence of having-been-seen.

The fountain is gone. The cobblestones are gone. The figure turning toward you is gone. Your hands. Your body. You said the words and the words did not catch.

You hang in the black, weightless, unanchored. There is nothing to do, because there is nothing to do it in.

**What do you do?**

## Turn 2

**Player:** I do not wait for the dark to explain itself. I press my hand forward into the nothing — feeling for the edge of it, for the weight that brought me here, for the shard that hasn't left me. If the words didn't catch, something else will.

**Game Master:**

Something is missing.

You know it is missing because there is a shape where it used to be. Your fingers close on nothing and you understand that nothing is not the same as absence — nothing is the place where something was taken back. The shard. You were carrying a shard. It held a message, a question, someone's last word pressed into glass. You remember the weight of it. You cannot remember the words.

You reach again. Deeper. The dark does not push back. It simply continues.

There is no edge to find. No keeper. No truth waiting to be held. The IAM NOTUM SUM was supposed to catch — you felt it rise in your throat and leave your mouth and the world was supposed to answer. The world did not answer. You are still here, still reaching, still holding the shape of what was taken.

**What do you do?**

## Turn 3

**Player:** I stop reaching. Instead I close my fist — not around the nothing but around the space the shard left behind. The message was in the glass; the absence is in me. Whatever took it left the shape of its taking. I hold that shape now and I do not let go.

**Game Master:**

Something is missing.

You know it is missing because there is a shape where it used to be. Your fist closes on the space the shard occupied and you understand — for the first time clearly — that the glass did not hold the message. The glass *was* the message. A question pressed into form. A truth given shape so it could be carried. And now the shape is gone and you are what remains of the question, the way a mold remains when the cast metal has been poured off and cooled.

You hold the absence. You do not let go.

The dark does not answer. The dark does not need to. You came here with words that did not catch and now you are what is left when a thing is taken back — not empty, not destroyed, just *post-something*. The shape of the shard is yours now because the shard is not.

You try to remember the message. The message was for someone. The someone is not here. The message was about something that happened before you came to the blue and after you left wherever you came from. The words do not come back. The fact of the words — that there were words, that they mattered, that someone spent their last moment pressing them into glass — that stays.

The dark has no edges. You have no edges. You are holding the shape of a question that has nowhere to go.

**What do you do?**

## Turn 4

**Player:** I open my hands. If the question was pressed into glass so someone could carry it, and the glass is gone, and I am what the mold holds after the metal pours off — then I pour it out. I give the dark what remains of a person's last intention. I let it go and I do not reach for the shape after.

**Game Master:**

You open your hands.

There is nothing to pour. The dark does not receive because the dark does not have a place to put things — it has only the ongoing of itself. You thought there was something in you worth giving back. A residue. A last intention pressed into the shape of a person who carried it. But the shape is the wrong word. The shape was never a container. The shape was only the carrying. And the carrying is over.

The last intention was not yours. It was pressed into glass by someone who had run out of time. They gave it to the glass and the glass gave it to the blue and the blue gave it to whatever shaped you. You are not the intention. You are the shape of the giving, not the give. And now you are opening your hands in a dark that does not need what you are offering because the dark is not a place things go. The dark is a place things stop.

You feel the absence of the absence. For a moment there was something missing and now even that is gone. The shard was the shape of a question. The question is gone. The shape of the question is gone. The memory of the shape of the question is following. You will be asked someday what you held here and you will not remember. Not because you were told to forget. Because there was never anything to remember. You came for a truth and the truth was already used up before you arrived.

The dark does not answer. The dark does not refuse. The dark simply continues to be what it is — the place after the place where something was. You sit in it. You do not reach.

There is nothing to reach for.

The dark goes on.
