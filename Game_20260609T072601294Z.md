The blue is everything. It is before the world had a floor, before the word "up" meant anything. You drift — or you are drifted — the distinction doesn't apply yet. There is no current because there is no water to move through; there is only the blue and the faint sense of a shape you are almost holding, something you couldn't name if you tried, something that is yours anyway. The hum of anything else is very far away, or very close, or not real yet. Somewhere above — or beyond, or before — a voice is forming. You don't know what it is saying yet. You will.

Show me who you are.
