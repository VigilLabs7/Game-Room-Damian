The blue has no bottom. You drift in it, weightless, formless — awareness without a body to hold it. Somewhere ahead, or behind, or to the side that doesn't exist yet, a truth sits lodged in the nothing: a single concept you are clinging to without knowing you are clinging to anything at all. The blue presses in from every direction, and you are alone in it, and you have always been alone in it, and the silence here is so complete that even the thought of sound has no meaning.

What do you reach for?

*— What do you reach for?*

## Turn 1

**Player:** I reach for the nine. Not the coins — the weight of them, the count, the shape of what I owe and what I carry. The debt is not a hole; it is the material. I close my fist around it and the blue learns an edge. Every debt is honored. Show me where the first course goes.

**Game Master:**

The blue answers. Not with sound — there is no sound here — but with *resistance*, the way a current meets a hand dipped into it. What you reach for is not empty. The nine sit in the nothing like a counted thing, like a sum already paid or already owed, and when your awareness closes around them they become *more real* than the blue that holds them. The weight of it floods back: not the coins themselves but the *shape* of what you carry, the count of what you owe, the particular gravity of a debt that has not yet been paid or forgiven. You feel it in the space where a chest would be.

The blue learns an edge. Where before it pressed in all directions without texture, without preference, now it has a *shape to avoid* — the shape of what you reached for. You are not formless anymore. You are a form that does not yet know its own edges, holding a shape the blue cannot swallow. You are almost something.

Almost. The surface has not broken. The blue still presses from every direction and you are still drifting in it, aware, orienting, but the reach has given you a *handle* — a counted thing, a weight, a debt acknowledged. The formless watches you with what might be curiosity or patience. You are holding on. You have not yet let go into the world.

What do you do?

## Turn 2

**Player:** I close my fist and push. The nine are my counterweight — I press down on what I carry and the blue gives way beneath me. I fall toward the world, toward the first course. The debt holds me steady as I drop.

**Game Master:**

The fall is not fast.

That is the first thing you know about it — the descent is controlled, deliberate, the way a stone sinks through water rather than the way a body drops through air. The blue parts beneath the weight of what you carry and you go with it, with the nine pressing down and the blue pressing up and the space between them closing around you like a hand closing around a found thing.

The debt holds you steady. That is not a metaphor. The nine are the weight that guides the fall, and the fall is not a surrender — it is a placement. You are being set down. You are being put somewhere. The blue was the space between the world and the idea of the world, and you have crossed it, and what you brought with you is what you are being placed into the world with.

Sand.

Warm. Fine. Real. It takes the shape of where you land — knees, palms, the weight settling, the nine pressing into the grains beside you. The blue is gone. The silence is gone. What replaces them is the sound of wind over flat ground and the immediate, impossible heat of a sun that has not moved since it arrived.

You are on your knees in the desert. The debt is still in your hand. Your other hand presses flat against the sand and you push yourself upright. Your knees are not hurt — there is no leg injury here, no tumble, no sharp arrival. You came down the way a coin comes down when it is placed rather than dropped. The debt honored you as you fell.

Ahead, the sand shifts in the wind. Something glints at the far edge of what you can see — not a structure yet, not a post. A point of light, catching.

You are not barely holding on. You are here.

**What do you do?**
