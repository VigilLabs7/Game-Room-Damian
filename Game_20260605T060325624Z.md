# The Ocean of Unbecoming

The blue goes every direction. No surface. No floor. No horizon — just the same colour in every direction, the same nothing above you and below you and around you. You are aware of yourself the way you might be aware of a word you can't quite remember: present, but not solid.

You are not alone. You are alone. Every drifter here is solitary, and even when two of you are technically close you cannot perceive each other. The blue does not allow it. You drift.

There is nothing here except you and the faintest thing — a single concept, taking shape. Not yet a word. Not yet a choice. Something you are holding without knowing you are holding it. The only truth that survived the crossing.

The blue is deep. There is nothing in it but you.

What do you reach for?
