# The Ocean of Unbecoming

The blue goes every direction. No surface. No floor. No horizon — just the same colour in every direction, the same nothing above you and below you and around you. You are aware of yourself the way you might be aware of a word you can't quite remember: present, but not solid.

You are not alone. You are alone. Every drifter here is solitary, and even when two of you are technically close you cannot perceive each other. The blue does not allow it. You drift.

There is nothing here except you and the faintest thing — a single concept, taking shape. Not yet a word. Not yet a choice. Something you are holding without knowing you are holding it. The only truth that survived the crossing.

The blue is deep. There is nothing in it but you.

What do you reach for?

## Turn 1

**Player:** The debt carries me forward — IAM NOTUM SUM. I arrive at the fountain with the weight still in me, the nine still breathing, Maro's hands still flexing into freedom. Every debt is honored. That is what I carry into the blue: not an absence, but a presence. Not a hole but a holding. I open my hands and I let the word take shape — the way through, the threshold, the one who stays standing so the crossing can happen. The blue receives what I give it. I am not the bridge. I am what the bridge left behind.

**Game Master:**

The world goes black.

Not the absence of light. The absence of having-been-seen.

The fountain is gone. The cobblestones are gone. The figure turning toward you is gone. Your hands. Your body. You said the words and the words did not catch.

You hang in the black, weightless, unanchored. There is nothing to do, because there is nothing to do it in.

The debt. You called yourself the debt. You carried nine. You carried Maro. You opened your hands and you let the word take shape and the blue received what you gave it and then the blue was gone and so were you.

You are still here. That is the only thing still here.

What do you do?

## Turn 2

**Player:** I stop waiting to be given a shape. The debt brought me here with my hands full — I did not come empty. I curl forward into the black, the way a man curls toward a door he cannot see, and I push. Something gives. Not a surface — a difference. I am still here, and still holding what I carried, and the black has to answer for that.

**Game Master:**

Something is missing.

You know it is missing because there is a shape where it used to be. The nine you carried — nine people, nine debts, nine futures you held up so the crossing could happen — there is a space in the black where they were. You can feel the edges of it. The weight of them was real. Maro's hands flexing into freedom was real. You pushed and something gave — you felt it — but the giving was not a door. It was a hollowing. The black opened to receive what you put into it and then the black closed around the absence and you are still here and the debt is still yours and the nine are still not.

You reach for them. Your hands are still your hands — you can feel them, the fingers curling, the palm reaching — but when they close on what you were carrying there is nothing there. The shape of the nothing. That is what pushes back. That is what has weight here.

The black does not answer your debt. The black does not answer anything. But something in the black knows you are waiting, and the waiting itself is the second shape — the one that was here before you started, the one that will be here after you stop.

You are still here. You are still holding. The black has to answer for that.

What do you do?
